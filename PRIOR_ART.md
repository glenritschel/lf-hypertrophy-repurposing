# Prior art & candidate rationale

Honest positioning for the nintedanib / pirfenidone → ligamentum flavum (LF) hypertrophy hypothesis. This is why the work is **published openly rather than patented**.

## The candidates and why

The cross-platform-replicated signature is a matrix-depositing fibroblast program (fibrillar collagens, small leucine-rich proteoglycans, HTRA1, MFGE8, POSTN, FN1, S100A4) — the effector program of fibroblast-to-myofibroblast activation, downstream of TGF-β1/Smad and PDGF/FGF signaling.

- **Nintedanib (lead).** Approved for IPF, systemic-sclerosis-associated ILD, and progressive fibrosing ILD. Inhibits PDGFR, FGFR, and VEGFR — fibroblast-activation receptors, plus (via VEGFR) the angiogenic coupling implicated in LF hypertrophy — all upstream of the replicated collagen/matrix program.
- **Pirfenidone (companion).** Approved for IPF. Suppresses TGF-β1 and collagen synthesis, directly upstream of the replicated program.

Both are amenable to local intra-ligamentous / peri-ligamentous depot delivery.

Note: connectivity mapping (LINCS L1000) did **not** nominate these drugs. Gentle, pleiotropic anti-fibrotics produce weak L1000 signatures and are structurally disfavored by connectivity ranking, which instead surfaces cytotoxic/anti-proliferative perturbagens. The nomination here is mechanism-based; the replicated signature is evidence that the drugs' target program is present and active in LF, not the discovery method.

## Prior-art landscape

- **Anticipation (novelty): clean.** No published study has tested nintedanib or pirfenidone in ligamentum flavum or LF hypertrophy, and no patent claims either drug for any spinal indication.
- **Nearest art — post-surgical epidural fibrosis (a different disease):** both drugs have precedent for *epidural fibrosis* (post-laminectomy scar), including a locally-delivered **pirfenidone spinal hydrogel** (HAMA) for epidural adhesions and rat laminectomy studies of both drugs. Epidural fibrosis is acute, iatrogenic scar in the epidural space; **LF hypertrophy is chronic, degenerative thickening of the ligament body itself** — a distinct pathology, trigger, tissue, and delivery target (intra-ligamentous vs epidural-space barrier).
- **Closest patent:** US 2024/0026359 A1 (University of Pittsburgh / UPMC, pending) claims the LF-hypertrophy indication with local delivery — but only for a **microRNA (miR-29a)** agent, not small molecules.

## Why publish, not patent

The therapeutic concept (a known anti-fibrotic for another fibrotic tissue) is mechanistically obvious, and adjacent prior art exists in epidural fibrosis. A defensible claim would have to be narrow — a specific intra-ligamentous depot formulation and dosing that produces measurable LF regression — which requires wet-lab formulation and efficacy data that a purely computational program cannot generate. Open publication establishes the finding, invites the laboratories that can test it, and serves the translational goal better than a narrow, hard-to-support filing.
